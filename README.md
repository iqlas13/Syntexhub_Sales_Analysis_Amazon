# Syntexhub_Sales_Analysis_Amazon

# 📊 Sales Analysis using Amazon Product Dataset

## 🏢 Internship Project – Syntexhub  
This project is part of my **Data Science Internship at Syntexhub** and is submitted as **Project 1 (Sales Analysis)**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Sales analysis is essential for understanding product performance, customer preferences, and pricing strategies.  
In this project, Amazon product sales data is analyzed to extract meaningful insights related to pricing, discounts, popularity, and category-level performance using Python.

The project follows a structured data analysis approach similar to real-world business analytics tasks.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze retail sales data to answer key business questions  
- Identify top-selling and high-performing products  
- Study pricing and discount patterns  
- Perform category and sub-category level analysis  
- Visualize insights and provide business recommendations  

> **Note:**  
> KPIs and insights are derived based on the available attributes of the dataset.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
**Dataset Used:** Amazon Product Sales Dataset  

### Key Features:
- Product ID and Product Name  
- Category and Sub-Category  
- Actual Price and Discounted Price  
- Discount Percentage  
- Customer Rating and Rating Count  

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
1. What is the overall sales trend based on pricing and discounts?  
2. Which are the Top 10 products by estimated sales value?  
3. Which are the most selling products based on customer popularity?  
4. Which is the most preferred ship mode? *(Handled as a dataset limitation)*  
5. Which are the most profitable categories and sub-categories?  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Matplotlib  
- Seaborn  
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Discount strategies play a major role in product sales performance  
- Products with higher customer engagement tend to perform better  
- A small number of products contribute significantly to estimated sales  
- Electronics and appliance categories dominate high-value segments  
- Dataset limitations were identified and documented professionally  

---

## 📈 Visualizations
The project includes:
- Pricing and discount trend analysis  
- Top 10 products by estimated sales  
- Most selling products based on popularity  
- Category and sub-category performance charts  

All charts are:
- Colored for clarity  
- Saved automatically for reporting  
- Optimized for readability  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on top-selling and highly rated products  
- Optimize discount strategies to maximize customer engagement  
- Prioritize inventory planning for high-performing categories  
- Collect additional data (orders, regions, shipping details) for deeper analysis  

---

## ⚠️ Dataset Limitations
- No order date (seasonality analysis not possible)  
- No region information  
- No quantity or profit column  
- No shipping or delivery attributes  

These limitations were handled transparently without assumptions.
